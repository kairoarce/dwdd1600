Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using technology and the internet in a safe, responsible, respectful, and ethical way.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, computers, and the internet.

What is AUP?

It is a set of rules that explain how people are allowed to use technology, computers, networks, or the internet at a school, workplace, or organization.

What is plagiarism?

Plagiarism is when you use someone else's words, ideas, or work and present them as your own without giving credit.

What is academic dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is when a student cheats or uses unfair methods to gain an advantage in schoolwork.

What are the rules for AI-generated content?

AI-generated content should be used honestly, responsibly, and according to your teacher's or school's rules.
