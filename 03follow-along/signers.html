Who Were the Signers?

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence include

John Hancock
President of the Continental Congress; his large, prominent signature became iconic.
Thomas Jefferson
Principal author of the Declaration and later the 3rd president of the United States.
Benjamin Franklin
Famous scientist, inventor, diplomat, and statesman; he was 70 when he signed.
John Adams
Major advocate for independence and later the 2nd president of the United States.
Samuel Adams
Influential Massachusetts revolutionary and cousin of John Adams.
John Witherspoon
The only college president to sign the Declaration.
George Walton
Georgia delegate who signed the Declaration despite being relatively young.
George Washington
Important correction: Washington did not sign the Declaration. He was commanding the Continental Army at the time.

each of these individuals played a significant role in the founding of the United States and is remembered for their courage in the signing of the Declaration, an act considered treasonous by the British crown.
